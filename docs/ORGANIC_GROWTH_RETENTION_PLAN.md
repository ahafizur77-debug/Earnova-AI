# Earnova AI — Organic Discovery, Activation & Retention Plan

**Status:** Product plan and metadata groundwork only. This is not evidence of implemented growth systems, real analytics, or a public launch. Keep changes on the review branch; do not publish or merge without approval.

## Product promise: give people a reason to come back

Earnova should earn repeat visits by helping users make progress—not by trapping attention. The strongest positioning is **social connection + useful progress + trustworthy opportunities**.

### 1. One clear first-session win
- Offer a short optional onboarding: choose goals (learn a skill, find work, build a creator profile, meet a community), language, and topics.
- Let people explore before demanding unnecessary personal data.
- Within the first session, help them complete one useful action: save a learning plan, improve a short bio, find a relevant community, or bookmark a verified opportunity.
- Explain what is demo data and what is real. Do not show fake live counts, fabricated job availability, or invented AI results.

**Measure:** first useful action completed, time to first value, onboarding completion, and voluntary return—not just account creation.

### 2. A healthy daily progress loop
- **Discover:** show a small set of relevant people, topics, and opportunities with a plain-language “why this is shown.”
- **Do:** offer a daily 5–15 minute action such as practising a skill, improving a portfolio, preparing for an interview, or sharing a small win.
- **Save progress:** let users keep their plan and resume where they left off across devices once secure accounts and sync exist.
- **Reflect:** offer an optional weekly recap of work completed and next steps.
- **Connect:** recommend a relevant circle or mentor only when it matches stated interests.

Use optional reminders, quiet hours, digest frequency controls, and a one-tap disable setting. No guilt messages, fake urgency, infinite notification loops, or penalties for missing a day. Streaks must be optional and forgiving.

**Measure:** meaningful weekly active users, completed learning actions, saved opportunities, useful connections, and user-reported value.

### 3. Build things people naturally share
- Create shareable progress cards, skill milestones, creator portfolios, public profile links, and community/event invitations.
- Let users preview exactly what will be public and remove private information before sharing.
- Use a real referral link only after accounts, attribution, anti-abuse rules, and privacy disclosures are implemented.
- Never auto-post, upload contacts, send invitations, or message friends without a clear user action.

**Measure:** voluntary shares, visits from shares, completed signups from shares, and the percentage of referred users who get a real first-session win.

### 4. Make Earnova discoverable on search and social
- Give the public landing page a clear, honest description and unique page title.
- When backend pages exist, create indexable public pages for public profiles, communities, skills guides, creator portfolios, and verified opportunities—with user consent and privacy controls.
- Publish genuinely useful, original guides: interview preparation, CV examples, beginner skill roadmaps, creator workflows, scam-spotting checklists, and local-language explainers.
- Add sitemap, robots policy, canonical URLs, Open Graph cards, and structured data only for real, public pages that comply with search-engine rules.
- Use human-reviewed content, accessible headings, fast mobile loading, captions, and low-data options.
- Do not create thin AI-generated SEO pages, fake testimonials, fake statistics, or misleading “earn money instantly” claims.

**Measure:** organic search impressions/clicks, useful landing-page visits, install/PWA-add success, and downstream activation—not traffic alone.

### 5. A distinctive feature competitors cannot copy merely by adding Reels
Build the product around **Progress Graph + Opportunity Match**:
- A user-owned map of skills they are learning, projects they have completed, and goals they choose to share.
- Practical weekly plans that connect learning tasks to portfolio proof and relevant opportunities.
- Matching based on user-selected skills, location/remote preference, language, and availability.
- Explanations for every match, freshness/source labels for opportunities, and an easy scam-report route.
- Private by default for sensitive career data; users control what appears publicly and can export/delete their information.

This requires real accounts, user-controlled data, verified opportunity sources, and tested authorization before being represented as live.

### 6. Trust creates long-term retention
- No paid-to-get-a-job offers, fake rewards, guaranteed income claims, or hidden payout conditions.
- Verify employers and campaigns before promotion; show source, date checked, eligibility, fees, and payout status.
- Give users block, mute, report, appeal, notification, export, and deletion controls.
- Use recommendation diversity and explicit user controls; do not optimize solely for time spent.
- Use privacy-preserving, consent-aware analytics with retention limits. Never collect sensitive data just because it might help ranking.

### 7. Launch sequence (free-first)
1. **Now — prepare:** improve honest landing-page metadata and document the growth hypothesis. No paid ads required.
2. **Next — validate privately:** ask a small group of real testers to try the prototype; record where they get confused and which action is genuinely useful. Do not present demo jobs/counts as real.
3. **Foundation:** secure auth, database APIs, privacy, moderation, and account deletion; run two-account authorization tests.
4. **Activation beta:** ship onboarding, user-selected goals, a useful first action, shareable public cards, and optional reminders only after backend support exists.
5. **Discovery:** publish original help pages and index only safe, public, useful content. Add real analytics with consent and a privacy notice.
6. **Improve from evidence:** run small experiments, compare retention and usefulness, and keep/roll back changes based on results.

## Initial experiment scorecard

| Question | Signal | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Do people understand Earnova? | Landing-to-first-action rate | No deceptive copy |
| Does onboarding help? | First useful action within session | Skip option and data minimization |
| Is content useful? | Saves, helpful votes, return to saved item | Hide/mute and chronological feed |
| Do people invite others? | Voluntary shares that lead to activation | No contact scraping or spam |
| Do users return? | Week-1 / week-4 meaningful retention | Quiet hours, easy opt-out, no guilt |
| Are opportunities trusted? | Verified listing views and successful applications | Scam reports, freshness, transparent eligibility |
| Is the app accessible? | Low-end Android completion rate | Performance, captions, language and accessibility checks |

Do not set numeric growth targets until a baseline from real users exists. Do not claim organic growth is guaranteed. The product should become worth recommending because it consistently helps users make progress.
